The original instrumental recordings produced by studio/music.py and the compositions they contain are offered under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They can be used in monetized videos without attribution. The synthesizer uses oscillators and noise and includes no third-party samples.

The Kaino catalog index points to instrumental MP3 recordings that Kaino publishes under the same CC0 dedication. See the [publisher's library and license](https://kaino.dev/library/music#licence). The index lists 492 recordings. PeakCut downloads and caches an MP3 when it is first played or used in a mix.

[Keynata Commons](https://carf-coder.github.io/keynata-commons/#license) dedicates its generated recordings to the public domain under CC0. PeakCut includes a snapshot of its public JSON catalog and can refresh it in the music picker. For karaoke entries, PeakCut offers the backing rendition.
