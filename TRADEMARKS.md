name or logo.** They identify the PeakCut project and its official service, and no permission to use them as your own
is given.

This applies to the name "PeakCut", the winking play-button mark, and the logo files in `web/brand/`
(`logo.svg`, `logo-light.svg`, `logo-email.png`, `favicon.svg`), wherever else the same artwork appears.

## You may

- Run PeakCut for yourself or your team with its name and logo as they are.
- Say truthfully that your project or video was made with PeakCut, or is based on it.
- Show the name and logo when writing about or reviewing PeakCut.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use the name or logo, or anything easily confused with them, for your own channel, social account, website,
  domain, app, product or company.
- Offer a hosted or modified copy to others under the PeakCut name or logo. Give it your own name and artwork.
- Suggest that your project or account is official, or is run or endorsed by PeakCut.

To ask for permission, open an issue in this repository.
